CORPORATE PROFILE & GOVERNANCE

About Mark Digital Services LTD

A newly established Nigerian digital and technology firm focused on helping businesses, organisations, entrepreneurs and individuals use technology to communicate, operate and grow more effectively.

A Practical Digital Partner, Not Just a Vendor

Mark Digital Services LTD is an incorporated Nigerian technology firm operating as a private company limited by shares.

Our registered business activities cover website design and software development, social media management, business promotion and digital branding, technology training and skill development, and the supply of ICT equipment and technology accessories.

Our approach is straightforward: understand a client's goals, recommend and build suitable digital solutions, and provide clear support that improves visibility, productivity, skills, and digital presence. As a growing company, we build long-term relationships with individuals, SMEs, organisations, and learning institutions that need dependable digital services.
